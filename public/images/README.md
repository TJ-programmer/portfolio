Drop Batman-style photos of you in this folder. The portfolio cuts them into a scroll-driven 3D sequence — each photo flies in from the side, lands with mouse parallax + breathing, then exits as the next one takes over.

Naming — EITHER style works, keep numbering consecutive from 1:
- `batman1.jpg` or `batman-1.jpg` — Hero / origin shot (appears at the top)
- `batman2.jpg` or `batman-2.jpg` — About / the vigilante at work
- `batman3.jpg` or `batman-3.jpg` — Skills / in the cave, suit-up
- `batman4.jpg` or `batman-4.jpg` — Projects / rooftop at night
- `batman5.jpg` / `batman-5.jpg` — Achievements / with allies or the Batmobile
- `batman6.jpg` / `batman-6.jpg` — Contact / back to the signal

Any subset works — the scroll splits evenly across however many photos exist.

Modes:
- Multiple photos (2-6): scroll-driven cinematic sequence.
- Single photo (only `batman1.jpg`): simple single-image 3D treatment.
- No photos: procedural 3D figure fallback.

Tips:
- Portrait / tall crop (2:3 or 9:16) works best; full-body or waist-up with a dark background.
- Generate all of them in ONE AI run so face + suit + lighting stay consistent.
- Keep them around 1200-2000px tall.
